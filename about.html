Who We Are

Enterprise expertise, built for the mid-market.

Cerebra is a systems integrator and IT services firm helping organizations modernize, scale, and get more from their technology investments.

Our Story

Cerebra Consulting Inc. was founded on a simple idea: enterprise-grade IT expertise shouldn't be reserved only for companies with enterprise-sized budgets. We combine highly capable consultants — many from top-tier consulting firms — with a low-overhead, offshore-supported delivery model to give clients real value without sacrificing quality.

We're a one-stop shop for mid-market technology, management consulting, and implementations — from filling an urgent staffing gap to leading a full-scale enterprise system rollout.

Industries We Serve

  • Manufacturing — long-term partners like Pella Corporation
  • Financial Services — tax technology and investment banking clients
  • Real Estate — global real estate services firms
  • Gaming & Entertainment — public gaming and lottery systems providers
  • Public Sector — government and public agencies
